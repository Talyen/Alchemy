# Art Generation Prompts — Alchemy

> Prompts for generating replacement/improvement art assets for the fantasy roguelite deckbuilder *Alchemy*.
> Each prompt is designed to be fed to an image-generation LLM. These describe standalone illustrations — not framed card game pieces.

---

## Global Style Reference

All prompts below share this visual direction unless overridden:

- **Style**: Hand-illustrated fantasy art in a grounded, tactile 2D style — oil painting / charcoal sketch reminiscent of an old storybook or bestiary. Not photorealistic, not 3D-rendered, not anime. Brushstroke texture encouraged on backgrounds and shaded areas.
- **Palette**: Grounded, atmospheric — warm neutrals, muted golds, deep rust reds, forest greens, smoky blues, bone/off-white. High contrast with sharp accent colors. No neon, no corporate gradients, no glassmorphism.
- **Lighting**: Directional, dramatic — candlelight, firelight, moonlight filtering through canopy, forge glow, etc. Strong shadows, selective rim lighting. Feels like a scene illuminated by a single light source in a dim space.
- **Composition**: Dynamic, asymmetrical. Subject clearly silhouetted against background. Medium-to-close framing for characters, close-up for items.
- **Output requirements**: 420×630px (3:4) for illustrations in the card slot, 720×960px (3:4) for enemies and characters, 900×900px (1:1) or as noted for destinations/homestead/game modes, square for trinkets.

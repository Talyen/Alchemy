# Conditional intent recovery

## Trigger

Do not auto-trigger from a path alone. First read the route-selected owner section and nearest assertions. Use this skill only if intent remains ambiguous for battle resolution, card descriptions/effects, material rewards, or persistence behavior.

## Steps

1. State the unresolved question.
2. Search the nearest tests for the named behavior; do not open whole suites.
3. If still ambiguous, inspect at most five relevant commits or one bounded line history.
4. Record the recovered invariant and its evidence before editing. Do not turn history into a general repository survey.

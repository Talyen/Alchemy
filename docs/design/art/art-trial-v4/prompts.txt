MANDATORY ART STYLE, verbatim:
"Illustrated in a bold, high-energy anime style in the visual tradition of the most kinetic modern action-anime studios — sharp, angular exaggeration, with dynamic motion implied through sharply cut diagonal shadow shapes rather than literal speed lines. Thick, confident black outlines of varying weight, with saturated, punchy colors and minimal color blending — shading built from two to three hard-edged flat color steps rather than gradients. Exaggerated dramatic perspective for maximum visual energy, sharp graphic highlight flares, and a high-contrast, almost graphic-design sense of color blocking rather than painterly subtlety. The environment itself carries the same bold graphic treatment as the character, filling the full frame."
FUNCTIONAL SHAPE: Outer card perimeter is a straight clean RECTANGLE, four straight sides and square corners. A modest continuous rectangular illustrated metal border contains the scene. It never has spikes, cutouts, jagged edges, ornamental corner projections, rounded silhouette or transparency. The inner wheel may be angular and dynamic; only its outer card frame is strictly rectangular. Full-bleed opaque artwork within the frame.
COLOR LANGUAGE: incorporate seven major keyword hues, each as a clearly separated substantial shape: Burn fiery orange, Freeze bright cyan, Nature emerald green, Consume saturated violet, Bleed crimson, Holy pale golden yellow, Physical icy steel silver. Dark blue-black ink shadows bind these colors. No rainbow gradients.
SMALL-SIZE DESIGN: One dominant readable wheel silhouette and a strong focal center. Use a few big structural forms and broad facets rather than fine engraving, filigree, ornamental ticks, tiny runes, dense sparkle dust or microtexture. Retain painterly-free illustrated richness through overlap, chunky physical depth, angular cuts and dramatic light/shadow. Read as an exciting piece of Alchemy card art at 161x215. No words, letters, numbers, labels, potions or flasks.
CONCEPT A — AWAKENED ASTROLABE. A massive enchanted gold-and-black astral wheel caught at a slight oblique angle, its near lower-left rim exaggerated toward the viewer and its upper-right arc receding, like a spell artifact awakening. Seven large organically angular elemental stones are sunk into the thick wheel circumference: flame-orange ember shard, cyan frost spear, emerald leaf-cut gem, violet void crystal, crimson drop-shaped ruby, pale gold sunstone, silver blade-cut stone. Each stone overlaps its broad socket and changes the wheel silhouette slightly, while staying legible as ONE connected artifact. One deep violet-black crystal heart commands the center, cut with one explosive white-violet graphic highlight. Few broad metal spokes, no needle-like embellishments. Saturated colored light casts large hard diagonal patches across the illustrated bronze-gold rim. Background is a bold near-black navy magical atmosphere formed by sharply cut diagonal slabs of shadow, interrupted by a few substantial orange and cyan elemental curls. Drawn with the same black ink as Fireball, not polished 3D gems. The wheel is dramatic, tactile and slightly asymmetric, never seven matching colored circles on a flat field.

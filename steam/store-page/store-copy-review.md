## Short description

Build your deck, fight alongside animal companions, and grow stronger between runs in Alchemy, a fantasy roguelite deckbuilder. Choose a hero and take on the Campaign, Labyrinth, or Wildwood Draft.

## About this game

Choose a hero, build a deck, and face enemies one card at a time. In Alchemy, each run is a chance to explore new card combinations, find equipment, and bring a companion into battle.

**Build around your hero.** Start with a distinctive deck and shape it as you play. Combine attacks, defenses, and effects such as Burn, Poison, Bleed, and Freeze to suit the challenges ahead.

**Bring a companion.** Fight alongside creatures with their own abilities, from a loyal Wolf to a Fox or Frost Whelp. Strengthen your bonds as you develop your Homestead.

**Choose your path.** Journey through the three-act Campaign, navigate the branching Labyrinth, or draft a deck for the Wildwood's endless boss gauntlet.

**Make progress between runs.** Earn talents, develop your Homestead, and collect Gear and Trinkets to prepare for future adventures.

# Current design inputs

Production artwork and audio masters live in the external Asset Library; importing and
optimization belong to [asset workflows](../WORKFLOWS-ASSETS.md).

The [current visual references](./visual-references.md) identify approved art anchors and screen-reference approval status. [Visual acceptance](../UI_VISUAL_REVIEW.md) owns rendered comparisons and the next-screen calibration pilot.

The Sound desk consumes [curated mappings](./audio-review/mappings.json) and
[approved cue mappings](./audio-review/approved-choices.json). Usage belongs to
[the audio workflow](../AUDIO.md#sound-desk). Keep these working inputs while their
tools and registration tests consume them.

Temporary experiments belong in ignored `output/` or `scratch/`. Keep pending
approval material only while the decision is open. Once selected work is installed,
remove superseded studies and duplicate sources; do not retain design archives.

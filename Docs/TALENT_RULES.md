# Talent Rules

Canonical detail linked from [GAME_RULES.md](./GAME_RULES.md).

## Talent manifests and progression

Talent tuning follows repeatability, not merely whether a condition exists. Frequent rewards stay small; once-per-combat opening grants and bounded opening/finishing windows can remain larger. Shatter adds 1 damage to positive player/Companion packets against Frozen enemies, and Corrosive does the same against Poisoned enemies; Exploit Weakness retains double damage. Active Stun/Freeze blocks further crowd control, and recovery starts their shared three-turn immunity. Do not balance around simultaneous Stun and Freeze as an ordinarily reachable state. Resource and Health threshold rewards can retrigger after returning across their threshold; they are not once-per-combat rewards.

Current scaling talents use numeric percentage fields. Run restoration rebuilds derived manifests from purchased talent IDs and Homestead effects without replaying opening rewards. Preserve valid current combat flags and progression; retire obsolete manifest fields and incompatible development snapshots under the [save baseline](../src/features/alchemy/shared/storage/MIGRATIONS.md#supported-baseline).

Armored Fists adds half Armor to Physical damage; Shield Slam adds half Block. Nature damage riders use the resolved packet amount and each roll independently. Ignite, Sanctify, Rust, and Tempered Guard use half Forge; Tempered Guard applies once to each positive Block grant, including triggered rewards and Overflow; full-strength Homestead or Gear permissions take precedence without adding Forge twice. Burn/Bleed sharing takes the stronger applicable Forge permission. Last Stand/Desperate Forge increase gains by 25% below half Health; Unrelenting doubles Physical damage strictly below half Health. Round fractional combat magnitudes normally, including zero results.

Homestead has four increasing tiers. Crystal Garden adds flat damage to existing
critical hits; it grants no Mana capacity. Leyline has a 5/10/15/20% chance to
waive Mana payment on an otherwise affordable play. It rolls only on a committed
positive-Mana-cost play, preserves unused cost discounts on success, and never
refunds Mana or previews a random free cost. A waived payment also spends no
Block through Winter’s Credit. Unaffordable attempts cannot roll.
Culinary adds flat healing to existing positive healing, including campfire rest;
Mycology adds flat Leech healing without granting Leech. Alchemy Lab adds flat
Potion damage/healing, without increasing draw counts or Mana restoration.
Blacksmith’s Burn contribution uses the strongest Forge percentage across
Homestead, talents, and full-strength permissions, never their sum. Shop removal
and Potion mixing apply Homestead discounts in both the price and transaction.
Detect Magic converts 3/6/10/15% of eligible Basic equipment outcomes to Astral
after ordinary progression weighting; it preserves total equipment probability,
Unique outcomes, and Astral eligibility. Existing generated items are unchanged.

Homestead battle keys in `HOMESTEAD_BATTLE_*_KEYS` are **added** onto talent values at battle start (`mergeIntoManifest`). Keep identity defaults (`potionPotency: 1`, `healMultiplier: 1`) off those key lists; homestead defaults are zero-based bonuses. Shop, campfire, victory, and collection UI do **not** receive the battle merge — if they need homestead, pass both ports or merge at that consumer. Do not assume `useTalentEffects()` includes homestead.

Incoming `receiveHalf*` resist talents use `scaleReceivedPlayerDamage` in `src/lib/battle/types/state-helpers.ts`. Enemy attacks scale once in `computeMitigatedDamage`; player DoTs scale once in `status-ticks.ts`. Do not also scale in `applyPlayerCombatDamage`.

Run-end keyword cards intentionally show level + XP bar only; the Talents screens own the unspent-point indicator. Dodge earns 1 XP per successful hero Dodge through `awardBattleDodgeXP`, using the battle counter delta in the same command that persists the resolved enemy turn. Ordinary card keyword XP and run-end multipliers still apply. Random damage grants the Physical keyword; a damage-type pool grants every possible type rather than its placeholder type. Never count combat text or award XP again while resuming a pending transition.

## Talent action rewards

### Wishes and Mana

- **Divine Intervention** — positive Holy damage has a 10% chance to Wish once for that resolved damage packet. Derived Talent damage cannot recursively roll the same Wish chance; existing Wish choice and queue rules remain unchanged.
- **Wish choices** — Discovery reserves one eligible undiscovered card when available and fills remaining unique choices normally. Generous Wish gives Wish cards a 10% chance to play fully twice. Powerful Wish upgrades both the immediate and matching scheduled effects when one description number covers multiple turns. It preserves Health/Mana costs and typed self-damage; shared Deal/Receive magnitudes remain unchanged so an upgrade cannot increase its penalty. Roads Not Taken rolls 10% after a valid selection and randomly grants one declined card from that offer through the normal hand-limit destination, including queued Wishes and full hands.
- **Mana restoration and rewards** — Wellspring checks Mana when the player ends their turn; enemy-phase gains or spending cannot change eligibility. Mana Moth then grants 1 extra Mana without increasing Mana Crystals. Ordinary restoration preserves existing overflow but cannot add beyond the cap. A Mana Crystal loss at the minimum of one crystal changes neither the cap nor current Mana; an actual crystal loss still clamps current Mana to the new cap. Arcane Mending restores 1 Health per Mana actually gained, including gains while nonzero; restoration at the cap grants no healing. Sanguine Overflow grants 1 Mana when Leech fills Health from below full.
- **Mana from Heaven** — every Wish immediately grants 1 Mana, including Wishes during enemy actions, scheduled effects, and repeated card effects. It uses normal Mana-gain healing and overflow behavior. Existing Gear Wish Mana retains its separate eligibility rule.

### Card plays and costs

- **Card base costs** — every playable catalog card costs 1 Mana. Effects may make a card free; valid saved cost overrides remain supported. Richer cards trade output, timing, conditions, or Consume rather than a higher base cost.
- **Card payment** — `card-cost-rules.ts` calculates cost, affordability, Block payment, and all cost-only allowance changes (including Unique Gear) once against the original state. `card-play.ts` applies that payment result; Unique damage preparation must not independently consume cost-only allowances. Combined damage/free-card opportunities such as Wildheart remain damage-owned. A successful play consumes those flags, prepares unique effects, then pays resources before resolving effects. Preview checks are pure and never cache payment for a later play. Saved zero-cost cards do not spend first-card-free perks, Quickdraw, Threefold Grace, The Knight’s Answer, or an armed cost reduction; an armed reduction survives when a first-card-free perk pays the cost.
- **Card play rewards** — Thermal Vent rolls 10% for 3 Forge on each Burn card play, before its effects; successful Forge uses ordinary gain bonuses and threshold rewards. Automatically played cards qualify; repeats and scheduled effects do not roll the play reward again. Faith Barrier is a Holy-damage reward, not a card-play reward. Sacred Shield remains a separate 10%-of-Block Holy damage bonus.
- **Automatic card rewards and discounts** — Dance of Blades grants Whistle's Companion action after summoning or buffing, and Mortar and Pestle damage after a Potion. These use the ordinary play reward paths. Mortar and Pestle also triggers for each duplicated Potion use. First-card allowances retain their established priority. After those allowances, Unique free-card effects and flat encounter/returned-card discounts apply before spending armed next-card discounts; a card already reduced to zero preserves Divine Favor and other armed next-card discounts.
- **Play rewards** — Iron Guard rolls 10% on each positive resolved Physical Health-damage packet and grants Armor equal to that damage. Thornskin, Bramblegrowth, and Photosynthesis independently roll 10% on positive Nature damage and grant Armor, Thorns, or Health equal to the resolved packet. Briar Patch and Toxic Pollen independently roll 10% to apply Bleed or Poison equal to that packet. Apothecary Membership reduces standard Potion purchase prices by 5 Gold. Ecosystem draws one Nature card before the ordinary opening hand. Armor Siphon steals only Armor remaining when its reward resolves, including after earlier play rewards deplete Armor. Quickdraw adds 1 generic damage to Archery cards while the player has no Block.
- **Card sequences** — Follow-through counts normal Archery card plays during the player turn and adds 1 damage to every original damage effect on the second Archery card. Repeated effects copy the resolved bonus; Companion actions, scheduled pulses, and damage echoes do not requalify. The next player turn clears the sequence.
- **Repeatable rewards** — Second Helping and Combustible reward each consumed card, excluding summon cards, without a turn cap. Combustible rolls 10% to detonate the enemy’s Burn after Consume effects. Last Supper grants 3 Forge only when the consumed card was the last card in hand before its effects and draws; automatically drawn-and-played cards never qualify. Leftovers independently rolls a 25% chance for 4 Gold on each Consume. A repeated card still Consumes only once. Overgrowth, Wildfire, Winter’s Grasp, Venom Strike, Jarring Blow, and Generous Wish contribute to one capped repeat decision for matching card keywords; a success resolves the whole card twice, pays Mana and Consume once, grants play rewards once, and does not roll again for the repeat. Resolve Forge and Combustible retaliation before later Consume rewards; a defeated hero gains no further rewards.
- **Free Follow-up** — Stun from any source, including Companions and delayed effects, readies the next card discount. It survives turn changes and is spent only when it reduces a card payment.
- **Consume reactions** — resolving a card twice still Consumes one card and grants Insatiable one bonus. Consuming Gear adds its rolled amount to existing Burn damage packets on cards with Consume. Rotgut adds 2 to Poison damage packets on Potions. Neither adds a separate hit or grants damage to a utility card. Both use normal card damage scaling and defenses. Feast doubles the healing effects of Apple and Bread only; their cleansing and other effects remain unchanged.
- **Talent progression** — Row eligibility requires every talent in prior rows. Purchased talents retain their IDs and progress when rows are reordered; see [save baseline](../src/features/alchemy/shared/storage/MIGRATIONS.md#supported-baseline).

### Attack bonuses

- **Cull the Weak** — damage effects of Leech-keyword cards gain an additive 25% damage bonus when enemy Health immediately before that effect is strictly below half maximum. Recheck each hit; use normal scaling, rounding, mitigation, and Leech healing. Companion attacks, unrelated talent hits, and status ticks do not receive this card bonus.
- **Attack bonuses** — Coordinated Strike accumulates for the Companion's next attempted damage packet; utility actions retain it. Card damage bonuses are consumed on the first attempted damage packet, including Dodge. Additional damage types resolve only if that packet deals positive damage. Equal-to-resource attacks also receive their bonus, including Opening and other Dodge-earned Physical bonuses.
- **Opening / Open Flank** — the armed Physical bonus applies to the next undodged attack of any damage type. Physical attacks include it in their main hit; other damaging attacks add a separate Physical hit through normal modifiers and defenses. Enemy Dodge preserves this bonus, and Companion actions do not spend it.
- **Hit rewards** — First Blood checks for no Bleed before the hit. Finish Him doubles Physical damage against an enemy strictly below 25% Health. Hawk Eye arms a single refreshable Crit readiness after a successful Freeze or Stun; the next positive player attack packet consumes it, while Dodge and fully blocked hits retain it and Companion/derived hits cannot consume it. Riposte arms a Physical-only guaranteed Crit after Dodge and preserves it through non-Physical, Dodged, and fully mitigated attempts. Snow Pack adds 1 generic damage to each Companion packet against an already Frozen enemy. Icebreaker grants Forge after the triggering Physical hit spends Forge. Desperate Siphon doubles Leech below half Health using the pre-healing Health check. Companion Bond rolls 10% for 1 Mana on each positive Companion damage packet.
- **Trophy Shot** — an Archery attack grants its kill Gold once after its complete damage packet, including secondary hits, Parting Cut, and detonations. Repeated hits cannot pay again for an already defeated enemy.
- **Pyric Gear** — adds its advertised percentage per Mana Crystal to the additive Burn damage multiplier, before rounding, pacing, critical hits, and defenses. It is not a flat fraction of the crystal count. Bloodember Pendant shares this bonus with Bleed.
- **Nature damage gear** — Bloomwoven rolls for each positive Nature damage packet that lands, including Companion attacks; Dodge, fully mitigated hits, and unselected chance branches cannot restore Mana.

## Talent event rules

### Selective feedback rewards

- **Eligibility and feedback** — evaluate resource and Health conditions before the triggering event's reward chain, with matching Talent/Gear amounts added together. An earlier reward cannot disable its matching counterpart or enable a different conditional reward in that event. Do not hide earned combat text; matching target/kind/stat events still merge normally. Each independent proc uses seeded world RNG, only when its source and basic target requirements exist.
- **Dodge payouts** — Feint has a 25% chance for 2 Forge; Lucky Foot a separate 25% chance for 4 Gold. Pack Weave has a 50% chance to activate the Companion; Bladedance has a 25% chance to draw and play a random card. Perfect Timing and Wardplate require no Armor, Nimble requires no Block, and Winded requires below half Health. Dodge counterattacks preserve bonuses reserved for the next card. Riposting has a 50% chance for its Physical hit; Fleeting requires the attacker to be Bleeding before Dodge rewards. Catch Breath, Footwork, and other unrelated Dodge rewards retain their existing behavior.
- **Stun payouts** — Riled Up/Forged, Guarded Counter/Steadfast, and Stun Surge/Arcane require respectively zero Forge, Block, and Mana. Matching Talent and Gear rewards add together against that single eligibility check.
- **Holy and healing payouts** — Blessed Leech requires below half Health and restores standard Leech healing from positive Holy damage. Faith Barrier rolls 10% to grant Block equal to that Holy damage, including Saintfall and other follow-up hits; Radiant Guard rolls 10% on each positive Block gain to draw a Holy card using the normal deck/discard draw path. Sun-Struck Shield reflects only attacks that deplete positive Block, using Block lost before defensive rewards refill it. Overflow converts only direct card healing's excess to Block, excluding Leech and incidental healing; Clean Slate retains its separate Leech eligibility.
- **Other payouts** — Coinmail rolls 10% on each positive Armor gain and grants Gold equal to the actual Armor gained. Bloodletting rolls 10% on each positive Health-loss packet and cleanses one harmful status for a surviving hero. Watchdog invokes the active Companion once when an enemy attack depletes positive Block while the hero is below half Health. Capture both conditions immediately after the hit, before defensive rewards refill Block or heal the hero. Desperate Wish and Wishborn require below half Health and Wishwoven zero Mana at the start of each Wish; Wishfire requires an enemy already Burning before that Wish’s rewards. Wishful independently rolls 50% for its Gold; Wishful Trinket rolls 50% to grant a reward, then uses its existing Forge/Armor selection. Jealous follows the [enemy reaction rule](./GAME_RULES.md#enemy-reactions). Resolve pending retaliation after each Wish’s rewards before beginning the next Wish’s rewards; a defeated hero earns no subsequent rewards. Talent-only Wish rewards retain their other conditions.

### Triggered and copied damage

- **Typed talent procs** — Entangle and Heavy Blows have a 10% chance to create equal-to-packet Stun. Lacerate applies equal-to-packet Bleed at 10%; Toxic Pollen applies equal-to-packet Poison at 10%. Scorching Light and Hemotoxin retain their existing typed conversions. Derived hits round their base amount, apply target resistance, Block, and applicable Armor once, and do not reuse offensive bonuses, pacing, critical strikes, or reserved card bonuses. Secondary Talent hits cannot roll the same new chance recursively.
- **Critical and copied damage** — Rupture detonates Bleed only after a positive Critical Physical packet. Parting Cut still copies half the resolved Physical damage as Bleed. Damage-matching Stun procs from Physical/Burn/Nature/Poison hits or Companion damage still copy the full amount. These effects use the same derived-hit resolver; their input damage has already been scaled and cannot apply Forge, critical strikes, damage bonuses, or fight pacing a second time. Viper’s Courtesy retains its separately documented typed-bonus behavior in [Unique items](./UNIQUE_ITEMS.md#combat-semantics).
- **Triggered damage** — Burning Wish resolves its talent damage through the typed trigger path, including Burn buildup, mitigation, Armor decay, and kill rewards; Gear-only Wish pulses retain their existing behavior. Mana Flare adds Burn buildup and decays Armor from its resolved damage. Icy Heart checks enemy half-Health reactions before kill rewards. Scaled damage against an already defeated enemy does nothing, including its damage rewards and reaction callbacks.

### Healing and Leech

- **Cleansing rewards** — removing multiple harmful statuses with a cleanse grants Sin-Eater’s Lantern and Cleansing Status healing once per status actually removed. Empty status slots grant nothing. Clean Getaway retains its single reward per Dodge.

- **Mending** — increases every battle Health grant once, including card healing, Leech, cleansing, Gear/Trinket healing, and the combined start-of-combat heal. Opening healing retains its unpaced amount, respects the Health cap, and triggers ordinary healing reactions. Apply its multiplier after source-specific healing bonuses; overheal rewards use the increased amount.
- **Deep Siphon** — explicit card Leech restores 10% more Health on every effect, including repeated and scheduled card effects. Incidental talent, Gear, and Companion Leech does not receive the bonus or spend a first-card opportunity.
- **Healing and reaction sources** — Blood Debt rolls 10% on each positive Leech-healing event and grants Gold equal to actual Health restored. Mana Siphon and Siphoning Gear each roll once per positive player Leech healing event, even at full Health; damage-only Leech-hit rewards remain limited to hits. Sanguine Overflow requires actual Leech healing from below full to full Health and grants 1 Mana, refreshed rather than stacked within that event. Affliction Siphon increases Leech healing against Poisoned or Bleeding enemies once, including normal Bleed ticks and detonations. Clean Slate reacts only to overhealing from card healing effects or a card's explicit Leech, including Gear damage repeats and echoes, never healing awarded by cleansing, gear, or talents.
- **Enemy healing reduction** — Necrosis and Mortal Wound each halve every enemy Health grant while their matching status is present, including Leech, Regeneration, Overgrowth, and Second Wind. Apply each reduction once, before fight pacing, with nearest-integer rounding. Mortal Wound’s matching status is Bleed.

### Armor, Forge, and Gold

- **Armor rewards** — Armor earned during battle is unpaced and passes through Last Stand, Reinforced’s 10% doubling roll, and Purification’s 10% one-status cleanse roll. Armored Surge rolls 10% on positive Block gains; Iron Guard rolls 10% on positive resolved Physical Health damage and grants Armor equal to that damage. Armor Siphon removes only the stated amount from the enemy before applying hero gain bonuses.
- **Reactive Guard** — a surviving hero gains its Block reward whenever positive Armor falls to zero, including normal decay, Sunder, Caustic, and Banshee's purge. Partial removal and removal from zero grant nothing; Ironclad prevents decay but not explicit removal. Caustic removes enemy Armor equal to each resolved Poison packet from direct Poison hits, natural ticks, and detonations.
- **Forge rewards** — Golden Crucible queues crossed Forge thresholds after granting its exact Gold-to-Forge amount. The shared battle reaction resolver drains those rewards before the action completes, including any resulting Cinder Skin retaliation. Ordinary Forge grants still resolve their thresholds immediately. Overheat doubles Forge gained while the hero is Burning; Intensify independently rolls 10% to double each Forge gain. Forge awarded by Vanguard’s Crest and Burn damage uses the same gain bonuses and threshold rewards. Emberforged shares one award per turn across card and Companion attacks. A hit spends its existing Forge before granting hit-earned Forge; Sunder reads the live Forge after a positive Physical attack and removes that much enemy Armor without spending it. Forged Bulwark grants 1 Forge when positive Block reaches zero, even if an earlier defensive reward refills it, then normal Forge threshold reactions resolve.
- **Sun-Struck Shield** — reflects 30% of the Block actually lost when an enemy attack depletes Block. Reflection respects enemy Holy resistance/weakness and Block, without attack bonuses, critical strikes, Forge spending, or a second pacing multiplier. Holy damage rewards still apply, but neither reflected damage nor secondary enemy reactions trigger another retaliation.
- **Gold rewards** — Seed Money rolls 10% on positive resolved Nature damage and grants Gold equal to actual Nature Health damage. Golden Wish rolls 10% per Wish for 7 Gold. Coinmail rolls 10% on positive Armor gains and grants Gold equal to actual Armor gained; victory payouts do not trigger it. Fetch adds its reward when a Companion is present at victory, including Wildwood.
- **Lucky Clover** — rolls on positive Nature damage, including Thorns retaliation and triggered Nature hits. Physical damage from Stun or Freeze Gear cannot grant its Gold.

### Turn rewards

- **Turn rewards** — Dark Recovery records zero Mana at player turn end and grants its extra Mana at the next player turn, allowing overflow and normal Mana-gain healing. Enemy-phase Mana changes cannot alter that recorded reward. Bloodrush rolls once for each positive Bleed damage event, including player hits, Companion/talent hits, natural ticks, and detonations; a success draws one normal card. Thaw Dividend draws when surviving enemy Freeze naturally expires; immunity and enemy death grant nothing.

## Distinct talent and card effects

- **Delayed cards** — Prayer costs 1 Mana and Consumes after Wish and healing. Stargaze deals 1 Freeze damage and resolves Wish 1 immediately; Bread restores Health immediately and does not queue a future pulse. Other delayed effects retain their source card ID, Consume property, and tags so card-specific, Consume, and Archery bonuses still apply. They remain battle-local, survive save/resume, and do not execute after battle completion.
- **Delayed healing** — scheduled card healing retains card-healing reactions such as Clean Slate. Check that the battle remains active before each queued pulse and subsequent Gear regeneration, including after turn-start Trinket damage. Plague Doctor’s Mask damage preserves bonuses reserved for the next attack; its legitimate kill rewards still resolve. Its cleanse rewards require removing the last Poison stack. Hero Thorns retaliation also preserves next-card bonuses.
- **Armor and Forge cards** — Stab deals 3 Physical damage while bypassing Armor reduction and preserving Block; it still respects Dodge and causes ordinary Armor decay on damage. Sunder removes enemy Armor equal to live Forge after a positive Physical attack; Caustic Jab removes fixed Armor before its Poison hit. Acid Potion halves current enemy Armor before its Poison hit. Burning Blade gains 1 Forge, then deals Burn based on live Forge; Forge is counted once in the Burn base, including with Forge-to-Burn talents, and ordinary Forge spending remains active.
- **Roll the Dice** — Consumes and draws the result of one fair six-sided die, subject to the ordinary hand limit and reshuffle rules; its fixed die faces do not change through numerical upgrades.
- **Potion potency** — Distillation adds 10% potency to consumed Potions. Brewmaster adds another 10% to consumed Mixed Potions; the bonuses stack additively and use normal nearest-integer rounding and hand limits. Feast doubles only Apple and Bread healing effects; their other effects are unchanged.
- **Terminology** — use “Deal N [type] damage”, “Gain N Mana”, “Gain/Lose N Mana Crystals”, “Restore N Health”, “Cleanse”, “buildup”, and “Draw a card”. Distinguish hits, natural ticks, detonations, damage bonuses, and separate typed hits. Health loss remains distinct from typed self-damage. New content uses the revised wording; valid saved effects and descriptions remain paired.

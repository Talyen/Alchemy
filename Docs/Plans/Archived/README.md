# Archived plans

Completed and cancelled execution plans may be retained here briefly for
handoff context. They are records of past work, not durable sources of product
or workflow policy.

Once the implementing work is committed, keep a short record only when its
rationale remains useful: the decision, reason, compatibility consequences,
verification summary, and implementing commit. Remove investigation transcripts,
obsolete approval instructions, and completed step-by-step checklists. Preserve
uncommitted handoff records until their owning work is committed; never invent
an implementing commit.

Otherwise delete the record after the next release; git history preserves it.
Move durable rules to their current owner before trimming a plan. Archived plans
must never be cited as the current behavior owner.

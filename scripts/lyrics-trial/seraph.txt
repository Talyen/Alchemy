The saint whose name is on your blade
Once knelt where you now stand.
You ask his shade to guide your sword.
I taught his living hand.

There now. Steady your breathing.
Take the stance they taught you well.
I have seen it fail in thousands.
I remember how they fell.

Be not afraid.
Stand in the light.
You prayed to see a miracle.
Now look me in the eyes.

I have kept my six wings folded.
I have veiled my burning face.
You mistook restraint for weakness.
Be not afraid.

A line of gold across my wrist.
One feather at your feet.
You stare as though you’ve slain a god.
You barely made me bleed.

Keep the feather, little champion.
Hold it tight against your breast.
That is all of heaven’s kindness
You have left.

Be not afraid.
Stand in the light.
You prayed to see a miracle.
Now look me in the eyes.

I have kept my six wings folded.
I have veiled my burning face.
You mistook restraint for weakness.
Be not afraid.

Watch the veil fall from my face.
Watch the white wings spread.
I will show you why your fathers
Prayed with lowered heads.

Be not afraid.
Stand in the light.
You prayed to see a miracle.
Now look me in the eyes.

All six wings are spread before you.
I have cast the veil away.
Now you know why mortals tremble.
Be not afraid.

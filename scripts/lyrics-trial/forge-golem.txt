Built in the breath of the bellows' flame,
the Forge Golem wakes.
Its rust-red heart still beating,
slow as a buried quake.

Strike and strike, but I do not fall
Rust is my crown, the anvil my hall
Strike and strike, but I do not fall
I answer with iron for all
Four shields I bear, unbreaking stone,
your blade will chip before I groan.
Bending iron on my skin,
I keep the old storm locked within.

Forge…
Soprano, sing of rust and rain,
sing of the marks I take in vain.
I was made to outlast flame,
I keep my name, I keep my name.

I wore black for every king.
Kept the crowns, forgot the names.
Hung their banners in the rafters.
Fed their letters to the flames.
There is room beside the fire.
There is wine enough for two.
You have come to kill a monster.
I have dressed myself for you.
Lift your chin.
Let me see.
Does it beat
So fast for me?

One more breath.
Keep your eyes upon me.
I will do the rest.
